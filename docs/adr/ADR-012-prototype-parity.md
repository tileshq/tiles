# ADR-012: The engine matches the gallery's cards on the pixel grid, compares every gallery counterpart, and takes an optional unit and note within intent v1

- Status: Accepted; the big number's change line superseded by [ADR-015](ADR-015-parity-round-2.md), which also compares gallery 44 with the new `stacked-100-bar`; for cards `tiles_chart` draws, its refusal of a note with nowhere to go is superseded by [ADR-020](ADR-020-dashboards.md): the note is left out and the result says so
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Amends: [ADR-003](ADR-003-chart-intent.md) (optional keys within v1), [ADR-010](ADR-010-engine-v0-1.md) (references and bounds)

## Context

The owner asked whether the engine's cards match the approved HTML reference to the pixel. A side-by-side check found strokes, points and ranked-bar value labels heavier than the prototype, an area label the prototype does not draw, connectors placed on half pixels so they read soft, and only four of the fifteen forms compared with the prototype although the gallery (`core/examples` in the prototype) has counterparts for eleven more. Comparing those eleven then showed the gallery's cards doing things the engine could not say: units on every number (31%, €8.2M), lines named at their ends instead of in a legend, month axes written `Jan 26` and thinned, a light axis instead of a value on each of twelve columns, a short callout tied to the named mark ("Still 52 min"), and the exception the title names leading the rows.

## Decision

### The pixel grid

The raster snaps to whole device pixels under the canvas's current transform, at every scale: level and upright strokes take a whole number of device pixels with both edges on pixel edges, and rect, track, card and border edges and text baselines land on pixel edges. Sloped data lines stay where the data puts them. Text baselines follow Chrome's rule (ascent and descent rounded separately, the odd leading pixel below), Vega's text offsets are rounded as Vega rounds them, the chart's top padding is rounded up as Vega's autosize does, and every card keeps the 4 px descent the HTML card leaves under its inline SVG chart. Playbook M6, checked by `crates/tiles-engine/tests/crisp.rs`.

### The gallery's marks

Points are Vega's symbol sizes (radius 3 at 70% opacity; a named period lays a radius 4.47 point over its own), a lone line is at 90% opacity, an area has no edge stroke and writes no value. A ranked row (bar, lollipop, dot, bullet) writes its value at weight 400, 4 px past the mark, the named row's in ink, which supersedes ADR-010's note that K4 colours that value with its mark. The labelled area the owner liked stays available as the opt-in `area-labelled` form. A spotlit line among several is 3 px and the rest 1.8 px without points; several lines are named at their ends, and a legend appears only when the names cannot fit down the plot. A slope names each series once on the left with its first value and writes the last value alone on the right. Past eight columns, a column chart draws a four-tick axis and writes only the named values. `YYYY-MM` periods read `Jan 26`; a point-based time axis thins them to a calendar step showing at most four, while a band form (column, grouped, stacked, waterfall) keeps one label per band, as the gallery's twelve-month column does. A dumbbell leads with the row the highlight names. The big number is 34 px.

### Two optional intent keys within v1

`unit` (`percent`, `eur`, `usd` or `gbp`) says what the y measure counts, and every value, axis tick and tooltip of that measure carries it: `31%`, `€8.2M`, `−€0.2M`. A scatter has two measures; the unit is its y's, and its x stays plain. Percent values are given in points, so a big number's change in a percent measure reads `+3 pts`, with no relative change beside it. `note` is one short callout of at most 48 characters, drawn beside the first highlighted mark on column, bar, lollipop, dot-plot, line, area, multi-line and dumbbell forms; it needs a highlight, never moves the chart (the card, plot, scale and marks lay out as they would without it, and the note takes only room they leave; a dumbbell's note stands in for the named row's second value, in that value's type, where it fits there; every other note is a muted label), wraps to two lines when one will not fit, keeps clear of every text and of every series' line, keeps a callout a row value's 4 px offset (`row_value_offset`) from the columns, bars, stems, dots, points, lines and other text so it never reads as glued to them, keeps a callout out of the row-label and tick column, within three of its lines of its mark and nearer to its mark (with that mark's own value and name) than to any other text, so it never reads as a row label, a tick or a neighbour's value, and a form that cannot place it on the card refuses it in one line rather than dropping it or drawing it off the card. Each line of a note sits on a knockout, a rectangle in the card's colour 2 px past its text, painted over the gridlines, guides and area fills and under the note, so no gridline or fill edge crosses its letters, while marks and other text stay avoided as before. Both keys are optional and change nothing about an intent that leaves them out, so every v1 intent draws as before and `v` stays 1. ADR-003's rule that a change of shape bumps `v` still holds for any change that alters how an existing intent reads.

### Every gallery counterpart is a reference

`tools/golden-refs/make_refs.py` draws eleven gallery specs exactly as the gallery has them (own rows, titles, annotations, heights) in both themes, and writes the same rows as engine intents to `crates/tiles-engine/tests/reference/intents/`, with the unit and note the gallery's card shows. The reference test covers fifteen cases. The four original bounds shrank to about 1.1 times their new measurements. A gallery case's distance is mostly what the engine's simple form leaves out (the gallery's taller plots, axis titles, reference lines and hand-set labels), so its bound records where the engine stands and shrinks as forms grow. One bound grew: the multi-line case's height difference went from 2 px to 24 px and its dark-theme mean rose, because naming the lines at their ends removed the 22 px legend row while the gallery's plot is 40 px taller than the engine's; the light-theme mean fell.

## Consequences

- Lines, gridlines and connectors read crisp in a LinkedIn screenshot at any device scale, and a test fails if a stroke lands on a half pixel again.
- A model states a unit and a short callout instead of baking them into the title; a note on a form that cannot draw one is refused, never silently dropped.
- The MCP tool's input schema, once generated from the `tiles-intent` types as ADR-003 sets out, gains the two keys without a version change.
- `stacked-bar` stacks vertical columns of totals while gallery 44 is a horizontal 100% bar, so that case stays far apart until a 100% stacked form ships.
