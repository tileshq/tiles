# ADR-037: A build without a design pack carries a plain theme of its own, with its own goldens, and CI tests both the plain set and the house set

- Status: Accepted; extends [ADR-036](ADR-036-design-pack-seam-and-deterministic-seal.md), whose build from source sealed the house tokens, and supersedes its sentence that `themes.rs` lists the three house greys; its `under_three_to_one` list in the house tokens is superseded by [ADR-042](ADR-042-house-greys-meet-three-to-one.md); its plain categorical order is superseded by [ADR-047](ADR-047-context-greys-and-no-red-category.md), which replaces terracotta and plum, peach and mauve with a slate violet and an indigo
- Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

The owner decided on 2026-10-19 how the open-core split divides the design: builds from the published source get a plain theme of their own, the forms and the fonts are public, and the house design tokens, the playbook and the goldens drawn with the house tokens stay private. [ADR-036](ADR-036-design-pack-seam-and-deterministic-seal.md) let a build take the house tokens sealed in a pinned design pack, but a build without a pack still sealed the house `assets/tokens.json`, so nothing checked what a build from published source would draw.

## Decision

- **The plain tokens.** `crates/tiles-engine/assets/plain/tokens.json` holds two themes, `dark` (the default) and `light`, written fresh rather than derived from the house file. The colours are warm stone neutrals with a teal accent in light and a cyan accent in dark; the categorical order is teal, blue, terracotta, plum and ochre in light, and cyan, blue, peach, mauve and gold in dark, the same hues in both so a series keeps its colour across themes. They were chosen with the maths `tests/themes.rs` runs: every categorical colour, the accent and the bad red stand at least 3:1 on the card, ink and muted text at least 4.5:1, and every two categorical colours measure at least 10 apart in CIEDE2000 as readers with deuteranopia or protanopia see them, with no exemption. A test fails if any plain colour appears in the house file. The marks are plainer: card corners of 8 px, one shadow layer, bar ends softened by 1 px, lines and points at full opacity, thinner lines and lighter areas. The type scale, the spacing, the plot sizes and the entrance timing keep the house numbers, because the terminal cell rounding (playbook C5), the note placement rules (M9) and the wrapper's 50 ms tick ([ADR-030](ADR-030-entrance-by-the-clock-on-every-route.md)) rest on them; changing them is a later design call.
- **Which tokens a build seals.** Without `TILES_DESIGN_PACK`, `build.rs` seals the plain tokens and the forms in `assets/forms/`; with it, the pack's tokens and the forms, as ADR-036 says. A debug build reads `tokens.json` from `$TILES_DEV_ASSETS`, and its forms from the `forms/` directory there when it has one or the embedded forms otherwise, so `TILES_DEV_ASSETS=crates/tiles-engine/assets/plain` loads what a build from source seals. The house tokens stay at `assets/tokens.json` until the public cut, read only by the `tiles-design` pin test and `scripts/test-house.sh`. `scripts/check-release-binary.py` without `--pack` takes its needles from the plain tokens.
- **Two golden sets.** `tests/golden.rs` compares a build from source with `tests/golden-plain/` and a build from a pack with `tests/golden/`, choosing by `tiles_engine::SEAL_SOURCE`. The plain set was blessed in this change, an intended visual change under [ADR-006](ADR-006-quality.md); the house set is untouched. `tests/reference.rs` measures distance to the prototype's card, which is the house design, so it runs only on a pack build.
- **Every theme the build carries.** `themes.rs` checks every theme. The three house greys under 3:1 are listed in the house tokens, as each theme's `under_three_to_one` slots with their colours, so the public test holds no house colour; the plain tokens list none. `Tokens::from_json` refuses an exemption whose slot the theme lacks or whose colour is not that slot's, and the test fails when an exempt colour stands at 3:1 or more. `rules.rs` lays every fixture scene out in each theme and fails unless every theme places the same items in the same roles, each coloured from its own palette; the sizes its rules check come from the tokens, not from house numbers. `crisp.rs` runs each test in every theme, `property.rs` hands its seeds to the themes in turn, and `golden.rs` already drew every theme.
- **CI tests both sets.** The `rust` job's `cargo test --workspace` builds without a pack, so it tests the plain set on Linux and macOS. A new `house` job runs `sh scripts/test-house.sh`, which seals pack 1 from the house tokens as the release job does and runs `cargo test -p tiles-engine` on a build that takes it: the house goldens, the prototype references and the exemptions. The `release-binary` job is unchanged.
- **The card rules page.** `docs/card-rules.md` lists every playbook rule id with its rule in one line and the test that enforces it, and no values. `scripts/check-docs.py` checks it as it checks the playbook, refuses a colour, size, time, share, ratio or decimal on it, and, while `docs/playbook.md` is present, refuses a page whose rule ids differ from the playbook's. A tree without the playbook is checked on the card rules page alone.

The rejected alternative kept `assets/tokens.json` as what a build without a pack seals and put the plain theme behind an opt-in, such as a `TILES_PLAIN=1` variable or a second tokens file chosen by flag. It moves no house file, but the everyday `cargo test` and the `rust` job would keep testing only the house look, the build from published source would stay unchecked until the cut, and the cut would have to flip a default in `build.rs` rather than delete files. Running the whole workspace a second time on the pack was also rejected: it doubles the longest CI job, and the CLI and MCP tests read theme names from the build, so they pass on either set.

## Consequences

- `cargo test` checks the plain set, and `TILES_BLESS=1 cargo test` re-blesses the plain goldens. `sh scripts/test-house.sh` checks the house set, and `TILES_BLESS=1 sh scripts/test-house.sh --test golden` re-blesses the house goldens. Switching between the two rebuilds the engine, since `TILES_DESIGN_PACK` changes the build script's output.
- Tests that switch theme pick one other than the build's default, since `dark` is the plain default.
- The board test in `crisp.rs` compares premultiplied channels with a tolerance of 8 levels: at the plain card's smaller corner, Skia's anti-aliasing moved two corner pixels by up to 4 levels of coverage between a card drawn alone and the same card drawn second on a board, while a card off by a fraction of a pixel moves an edge pixel by a hundred levels or more.
- At the public cut the private files leave together: `assets/tokens.json`, `tests/golden/`, `tests/reference/` and `tests/reference.rs`, `docs/playbook.md`, `scripts/test-house.sh` and the `house` job move to the design repository, and nothing else in the build changes. The add-ons' theme hints that name Obsidian and the links to the playbook in the definition of done, the README and ADR-007 are rewritten in the public-prep step.
