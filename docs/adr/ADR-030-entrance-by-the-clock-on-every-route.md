# ADR-030: Every route plays the entrance by the clock at 50 ms a frame, a pre-cut card grows with its block, and the entrance takes Emil Kowalski's strong ease-out

- Status: Accepted; its pre-cut card growing with its block, and the new picture placed before the old is deleted in the same update, are superseded by [ADR-044](ADR-044-precut-card-whole-from-its-first-row.md): the whole card shows from the block's first row, and a replaced picture goes a tick later; its pre-cut entrance is amended by [ADR-053](ADR-053-precut-entrance-waits-for-a-still-card.md): a moved whole card whose frame is over 64 KB places its next frame sent ahead instead of dropping the entrance
- Date: 2026-10-19
- Supersedes: [ADR-010](ADR-010-engine-v0-1.md)'s entrance (22 frames over 540 ms on the prototype's CSS curves); [ADR-017](ADR-017-early-card-and-entrance.md)'s run repeated every 50 ms and stopped after "the frames' time and half again", and its `TILES_FRAMES` for kitty only; [ADR-020](ADR-020-dashboards.md)'s 150 ms stagger capped at 600 ms and its frame count fixed per harness; [ADR-025](ADR-025-warp-cell-size-rows-and-laptop-check.md)'s card shown on the pre-cut route only once its block is whole

## Context

The owner tested v0.1.2 in Warp on a laptop on 2026-10-19. In Claude Code and Pi the chart "just appeared abruptly for both, no smooth natural animation and flow in". In OpenCode he saw "a flash of white and then abruptly the card appeared", and for the dashboard "the blank screen going all the way down before the dashboard appeared abruptly". He left the design to us: "We want speed, craft and a polished product and the best user experience."

The wrapper played an entrance only in kitty. On the pre-cut route (Warp, WezTerm, VS Code, herdr) it blanked each `[tile:` row as the harness typed it and placed the card once every row had been seen, in one go. A blank with no painted cell beside it took the terminal's default background, and the harness's row reached the terminal before its blank. In kitty, Claude Code's 22 frames were 26 ms apart while the wrapper ticks every 50 ms, and the run stopped at 1.6 times the frames' time where ADR-017 said half again. An evaluation of Emil Kowalski's animation skills (private research notes) measured this and specified the pre-cut entrance; its middle timing, variant C, was picked under the owner's mandate.

## Decision

- **Timing (playbook N1).** The tokens hold `frame_ms` 50 and variant C: the card fades over 300 ms on the strong ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` and floats up 10 px from a 0.985 scale over 400 ms on the settle curve; marks grow from 100 to 400 ms, then values fade in from 300 to 450 ms on the strong ease-out. An entrance has one frame per 50 ms step begun and the settled card: 10 frames for one card. Dashboard cards start 50 ms apart, at most 200 ms from first to last, always on a whole frame.
- **Reduce motion (N2).** `TILES_MOTION=on|reduce|off` wins; otherwise `tiles run` reads `defaults read com.apple.universalaccess reduceMotion`, where `1` means reduce and anything else, an error included, means on. `reduce` fades the settled card in over 150 ms in 4 frames on CSS `ease-out`, every card of a dashboard at once: the strong ease-out put nearly all of so short a fade into its first visible frame, which the `review-animations` skill flagged as a pop. `off` draws no frames, and the card shows settled. `tiles check` prints `motion: entrance on the <route> route, 10 frames at 50 ms`, `motion: reduce, 4 frames over 150 ms` or `motion: off`; `tiles doctor` prints the setting and its source.
- **Every animating route gets frames.** `tiles run` sets `TILES_FRAMES` (one card's count) on the pre-cut route as it does in kitty, and `TILES_MOTION=reduce` under reduce. The server draws frames `frame_ms` apart for as long as the entrance runs, so a dashboard's has more. Theme swaps stay kitty's (ADR-021): the server swaps only where no `TILES_ROWS` is set.
- **The clock picks the frame.** The frame on screen is `floor(elapsed / 50 ms)`, with the clock started one frame before the card's first showing: frame 0 is the card at no opacity, and stepping the first demo recording showed it as a tick with nothing where the block had been blanked, so the first showing is frame 1, and frame 0 is never sent on either route. A late tick skips to the frame for the time passed; past the entrance's length the settled card shows and the run stops. In kitty the wrapper shows each frame with `a=a,c=N,s=1` rather than running kitty's animation and repeating the run.
- **The pre-cut entrance.** The entrance frames go to the terminal ahead as images of their own (`a=t`), within 64 KB a tick, while the reply is typed. From the first showing each tick places the frame for the time passed and deletes the one before, new placed before old deleted, so no tick shows an empty cell; the crisp card ends it. A frame placed once is never placed again, since Warp keeps the old copy: a card moved or cut mid-entrance gets its current frame's rows cut fresh, never frame 0. A card shown before its frames are all in the terminal shows settled and drops its entrance. `TILES_PRECUT_SEND=tick` sends each frame as it is placed, for a terminal that will not place a picture sent earlier, and drops the entrance when a frame is over 64 KB, as a dashboard's is.
- **The card grows with its block.** On the pre-cut route each paint places a strip for the rows typed since the last, under the strips already in place; a moved or scrolled card is placed again as one strip, and while it is still being typed at most once a tick, what it sends counting against the tick's 64 KB; its band is encoded once per set of rows seen and sent again as it is when only its place changed. A card counts as whole, and its trace line says so, only once every row has been seen.
- **No flash.** A blank takes the background of the cell beside the block, else the conversation's, remembered from the last screen mostly painted in a colour of its own, never the default there. Harness bytes written outside an update of the harness's own go into the paint's synchronized update, so a typed token never shows before its blank.

## Consequences

- Variant C replaces the approved prototype timing. A frame-by-frame comparison of N1, variant B and C sits with the research notes; if the owner picks another, only `tokens.json` changes.
- While a block is typed during its entrance, each tick decodes a half-resolution frame and encodes the typed rows; a dashboard frame cut that way can run past the tick's budget and drop the entrance. A card typed while the harness scrolls it (`OpenCode`'s viewport) is placed again at tick pace, so its picture can trail its block by up to a tick; the laptop step measures what that costs.
- Which path Warp takes, frames sent ahead or `TILES_PRECUT_SEND=tick`, is open until the owner's laptop recording; none of this was seen in Warp on the development Mac, where kitty and the paint tests prove it.
- The painter's TLA+ model is unchanged: its clock already stands for "the entrance's length has passed". It still leaves out the pre-cut route.
- Tests: `crates/tiles-engine/src/motion.rs`, `crates/tiles-engine/tests/crisp.rs` and `rules.rs`; the paint tests in `crates/tiles-cli/src/wrap/paint/tests.rs` for the flash, the reveal, the clock, a late tick, a moved card and two cards' clocks; `crates/tiles-cli/tests/pty.rs` in kitty and Warp; the plan test in `crates/tiles-cli/src/harness.rs`; `crates/tiles-cli/src/motion.rs`; the motion and trace lines in `check.rs` and `doctor.rs`.
