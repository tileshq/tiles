# ADR-057: The wrapper blanks Pi's streamed closing fence, keeps pre-cut cards through a resize, and blanks the title on a waiting block's first row

- Status: Accepted; amends [ADR-052](ADR-052-block-inside-a-tiles-fence-again.md)'s fence blanking (from [ADR-043](ADR-043-block-as-plain-lines-and-fence-blanking.md)), [ADR-044](ADR-044-precut-card-whole-from-its-first-row.md)'s rule that a replaced picture goes a tick later, which now covers a resize, and [ADR-053](ADR-053-precut-entrance-waits-for-a-still-card.md)'s "while held its tokens stay blanked"
- Date: 2026-10-22

## Context

Screen recordings of v0.1.12 in Warp on 2026-10-22, one each for Claude Code 2.1.302, Pi 1.2.0 and OpenCode 2.1.2 on the pre-cut route, were read frame by frame against the sessions' traces. Three of the faults they show have causes in the wrapper.

- **Pi's backticks.** Pi's markdown renderer draws a closing fence after every code block, also while the block streams (`pi-tui` `markdown.js`, `case "code"`), so each paint has ```` ``` ```` on the row under the newest token row, indented two columns less than the tokens. The wrapper blanked a fence only above a block's first row or below its last (ADR-043, kept by ADR-052). The rows a card covers that its block has not typed yet are blanked from the tokens' column (ADR-044), so two backticks stayed left of the card and stepped down its edge one row per typed row: 24 positions in 0.77 s for the first card. Blanking the row under every token row was not enough on its own: a row took one blank per paint, and the not-yet-typed blank came first.
- **The blink on a resize.** Each recording ended with the terminal three rows shorter and back. `Painter::resize` deleted every pre-cut picture at once, and nothing went up again until the harness drew its screen. In Claude Code both cards were gone for 3 to 4 video frames (about 70 ms), then doubled for one. A fake harness in Warp on the development Mac that stayed quiet after the pty shrank lost its card for good. ADR-044 already deletes a replaced picture a tick after its replacement, because Warp shows nothing in place of a picture deleted in the update that places the new one; a resize had been left out.
- **A title alone on blank rows.** A block waiting for room (ADR-044) or held until its card stops moving (ADR-053) had only its 19-character tokens blanked. The first row's title text after the token stayed, in the harness's font, over blank rows, for 0.3 to 3.5 s before the dashboard's entrance.

## Decision

- **A fence under any token row is blanked.** The wrapper blanks a code fence line (`fence` in `crates/tiles-cli/src/wrap/paint.rs`) on the row right above a block's first row, as before, and on the row right below any of its rows, where Pi draws its closing fence while the block streams. Below a row that is not the last, that row otherwise holds the next token, so no other text is touched. A row may take several blanks in one paint (`Painter::blank_tokens`).
- **A resize keeps pre-cut pictures.** On `Route::KittyPrecut`, `Painter::resize` follows the new size and deletes nothing. Each card on screen is marked (`Painter::resized`), and the next paint that finds its block places it again under a new image id wherever the block now stands, even on the row it had, since the terminal may have moved a picture with its text. The old pictures then go a tick later, as on any move. Pictures already due to go keep their time. `Painter::resize` itself paints nothing, since its screen model drops rows from the foot on a shrink and a harness's redraw can put a card elsewhere. A tick that paints anyway (an entrance playing, a held card deferred, a stalled block) reads that model, so it places a resized card again from the model's rows, which in Warp can be the wrong rows until the harness draws its screen again; the old code did the same after `forget_placements`. The trace writes `resized to R by C; N cards kept until placed again`, and each card placed again writes its usual `placed` line. The other routes keep deleting their placements on a resize.
- **A waiting block's first row is blank to its end, within the card's drawn width.** While a block waits for room or is held, its first row, where the title follows the token, is blanked from the token to the end of the title, up to the width the card will be drawn at: its own width, cut at a side panel's edge and at text the harness wrote right of the block's other typed rows (ADR-020), as `Painter::draw` cuts it. The title is one run of text after the token's space (`block::lines`), so the blank stops at a gap of two spaces. The wait shows empty rows. The other token rows are blanked as before, token only. Text right of that width, such as a panel's border on every row of the block, stays during the wait and after the card goes up, and the card covers the blanked text once it is placed.

## Consequences

- In Pi the first card shows no backticks while it types. Before the block's first token row reaches the screen, Pi can draw an empty code block for a frame; with no token on screen there is nothing to anchor a blank to.
- A window resize leaves each card on screen until the harness draws its screen again; then the card is placed again, and for one tick the old copy may show under it, as on any move (ADR-044). A harness that stays quiet keeps its cards where the terminal put them, unless a tick paints while an entrance plays, a held card is deferred or a block has stalled; that paint places the card from the resized model, and the harness's next draw puts it right.
- A first row on screen with no row of its block under it has nothing to cut its card, so only the gap after the title keeps a panel's border on that row from being blanked; a harness that scrolls the row up before the next row comes, drawing only changed cells, never writes a blanked cell again. A title holding two spaces in a row shows its tail on the waiting row, and text one space after the title is blanked with it.
- A waiting dashboard shows only the reply's other text and blank rows until its entrance. What fills that space, if anything, is still open, as ADR-053 said.
- None of this has been seen on the laptop yet. The next laptop check records Pi's first card (no backticks beside it) and a window resized with a card settled (no blink).
- Tests: in `crates/tiles-cli/src/wrap/paint/tests.rs`, `precut_pi_streamed_closing_fence_is_blanked_left_of_a_growing_card`, `precut_a_resize_keeps_the_card_until_it_is_placed_again`, `precut_a_resize_places_the_card_again_where_its_block_stands`, `precut_a_block_waiting_for_room_shows_no_title` and `precut_a_waiting_block_keeps_the_text_right_of_its_card`.
