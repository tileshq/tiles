# ADR-053: On the pre-cut route a card that waited for room enters once it stops moving, a moved dashboard keeps its entrance, and the trace says when a card is missing

- Status: Accepted; amends [ADR-044](ADR-044-precut-card-whole-from-its-first-row.md)'s "a block with no room waits to be whole" and [ADR-030](ADR-030-entrance-by-the-clock-on-every-route.md)'s pre-cut entrance; its held block's blanking is amended by [ADR-057](ADR-057-precut-fence-resize-and-waiting-rows.md), which blanks its first row's title within the card's drawn width
- Date: 2026-10-21

## Context

The owner's v0.1.10 test in Warp on 2026-10-21 showed Claude Code's first card enter smoothly in place, while the dashboard after it and a repeated first prompt each showed blank rows under a lone title, then a fade on a card that climbed the screen with its title doubled, then a jump to the crisp card. The diagnosis, from the recordings, the session's trace and a fake harness on the development Mac, found the following.

- **The trigger.** Claude Code typed each block a row every 100 to 150 ms at the foot of a full screen, under its input box, so each row scrolled the screen up a row. The session's first card was typed under a near-empty screen and never moved.
- **The wait.** ADR-044 places nothing for a block that has no room under its first row for every card row. The dashboard showed only the title text after its first token for 2.5 s.
- **The entrance on a moving card.** Once the reply had scrolled the block up far enough, the entrance started at once. Each row typed after that scrolled the card again, so its frames landed on rows 27, 26 and 24, and the moved card left its old copy a tick under the new one, which showed as a doubled title.
- **The entrance dropped.** An entrance frame sent ahead is placed once, since Warp keeps the old copy of a picture placed again (ADR-030). A card that moved after its current frame was placed had that frame's rows sent again, but only within the 64 KB a tick may send. On the development Mac a dashboard's frames from the fifth on are 77,802 to 81,550 bytes and a line card's 21,698 to 29,150 bytes. So the dashboard, moved at its fifth frame of 12, dropped its entrance for the crisp card, while the line card survived its moves. The 64 KB limit came with ADR-030, when a moved frame was only the band of rows typed so far; ADR-044 made every frame the whole card.
- **The trace said nothing.** `tiles check` passed the session, since every card's trace had a `whole:` line, which means every block row was seen, not that the card was on screen. A block waiting for room, a card deleted because its block's tokens left the screen (how every OpenCode card went under ADR-043), and blanked tokens wrote no trace line.

## Decision

- **A moved card goes on to its next frame.** On `Route::KittyPrecut`, when a whole card's current entrance frame has already been placed and its rows are too large to send again in a tick, the painter places the next frame sent ahead and never placed, ahead of its turn (`Painter::frame_picture`). The entrance never steps back from the furthest frame placed (`Reel::reached`): when the clock reaches that frame it is already up, and the clock's next frame follows. Frames that fit in a tick are still cut fresh, as ADR-030 set. The entrance is dropped for the crisp card only when no later frame is left unplaced, or when the card is cut (rows off the screen's top, or columns at a harness's panel), whose bands are no frame sent ahead.
- **A card that waited for room enters once it stops moving.** A block that ADR-044 kept back for lack of room, whose card has an entrance still to play, stays back once it has room until its card stops moving (`Painter::hold_moving`): once it is whole, once its typing stalls for 1.5 s (`STALL`), or once a row is typed with the block in place and a tick (50 ms) passes with no move. Stillness alone does not count, since rows typed 100 to 150 ms apart leave the block still for longer than a tick between scrolls. While held its tokens stay blanked, as in ADR-044's wait. A card with no entrance to play, and a block that had room from its first row, go up as ADR-044 set.
- **The trace says when a card is missing.** Under `TILES_TRACE` the pre-cut painter writes:
  - `<id> waiting for room: needs N rows, has M`, when a block is first kept back for lack of room;
  - `<id> has room at screen row Y; held until its card stops moving`, when it first has room and is held;
  - `<id> shown settled; entrance dropped: <why>, K frames unsent`, where `<why>` names the frame and its bytes (`frame 12 is 81550 bytes`), the frames in the terminal, or the columns the card was cut to;
  - `<id> scrolled away; pictures deleted`, when a card goes whose first rows had already left the viewport's top edge, or a whole card whose last rows had left its foot edge, as under OpenCode's editor;
  - `<id> copy lost at screen row Y; pictures deleted`, when a card goes while all its rows were on screen, as when OpenCode drew its block's rows again as links.

  `tiles check` fails its `trace:` line when, of the placed, scrolled-away and copy-lost lines for a card the session made, the last is a lost copy, naming the card. Entrance lines that follow do not hide the loss.

## Consequences

- A block typed at the foot of a full screen shows nothing for longer: until it is whole, rather than until it has room. Typed above a five-row input box, a card of any height has room once all but its last 5 rows are typed, so the wait grows by those 5 rows, about 0.6 s at 120 ms a row. Then the whole entrance plays in one place. What a waiting card shows during the wait is still open.
- A card moved mid-entrance runs up to a frame ahead of its clock, and each further move while its frame is too large to send again takes it one frame further. A dashboard that moves while showing its last frame still goes to the crisp card at once, and the trace says why.
- A block that pauses mid-typing while held shows nothing until the next row or until 1.5 s pass, when it shows cut to its typed rows as ADR-044 set.
- A harness that types a row and scrolls in two separate writes can start the entrance between them; the first decision keeps the entrance going through the move that follows. The diagnosis's fake harness does that, and replayed through `tiles run --terminal warp` on the development Mac at a load average of 18, its 24-row line card started two rows before it was whole and moved two rows while it played all 8 frames. Claude Code writes a row and its scroll in one synchronized update.
- In the same replay the 38-row dashboard, typed a row every 120 ms at the foot of a full 52-row screen, waited for room, was held until whole, and placed every frame to the 12th with no entrance dropped; the reply's closing lines then scrolled it two rows. v0.1.10 dropped the same dashboard's entrance after its fifth frame.
- A card whose block leaves the screen in one write, all its rows at once, reads as a lost copy and fails the `trace:` line though nothing went wrong; a card that scrolls off a row at a time, past the viewport's top or foot edge, reads "scrolled away" and passes.
- None of this has been seen in Warp on the laptop yet. The next laptop check records the Claude Code prompts and the repeated first prompt: a later card's entrance plays in one place, the dashboard's through all 12 frames.
