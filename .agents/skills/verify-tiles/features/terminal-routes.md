# Terminal routes

`tiles run` detects the terminal (overrides first, then the environment, then the terminal's replies) and draws each card by one route: kitty placeholders, kitty pre-cut placements, sixel, or text. The wrapper finds the placeholder block on screen and draws over it, one copy per card (ADR-004, ADR-011); a card counts as whole once every row of the block has been on screen, across paints. In kitty it places the whole card on the block's first row and plays its entrance as one kitty animation (ADR-017); on the pre-cut route it places the whole card on the block's first row too and plays its entrance a frame per tick (ADR-030, ADR-044). Every route picks the frame shown by the clock, 50 ms a frame.

## Sub-features

- `route-detect` asks the terminal (kitty graphics query, XTVERSION, `CSI 16 t`, `OSC 11`, device attributes) and reports it in `tiles doctor`.
- `route-cell-size`: `TILES_CELL_PX` when set, else the `CSI 16 t` reply, else the window size over its rows and columns, read as points (times the scale) in Warp, which reports points at every font, and in any other terminal when it gives a cell under 10 CSS pixels tall; `tiles doctor` prints it as `cell size: <W>x<H> device px, from <source>` (`detect::cell_px`).
- `route-fit-rows` (kitty-precut only): `tiles run` passes `TILES_ROWS`, and a card taller than the rows less 10 is drawn on fewer cells, as if each were larger, so its headline is never above the screen (`card::draw::fit_rows`). The sixel route gets no `TILES_ROWS`, since a sixel is drawn at its pixel size and a fitted card would spill past its block; a tall card there is cut at the screen's edge as before (`only_the_precut_route_gets_the_rows`).
- `route-kitty-placeholders` (kitty, Ghostty, iTerm2): one virtual placement and Unicode placeholder cells.
- `route-kitty-precut` (Warp, WezTerm, VS Code, herdr): placed pictures; a partly visible card is a new picture of its visible rows; a moved card goes up under a new id at a higher `z` over the old one, which is deleted a tick later, never in the update that places its replacement; a whole card in several strips goes up again as one; each session numbers its pictures from an id drawn from its token, above every card's own 24-bit id (ADR-044).
- `route-precut-reveal` (pre-cut): the whole card goes up on the block's first row, over every row the block will take, and the rows typed under it place nothing; the rows not typed yet are blanked like tokens and written back once the card leaves them, and only the typed rows bound the card's width; a block with fewer rows under its first, down to the screen's last, than it will take places nothing until it has room or is whole, and once it has room, with an entrance still to play, until its card stops moving: whole, stalled, or a tick after a row typed with the block in place (ADR-053); a block that goes 1.5 s without a new row is cut to its typed rows and gives the lines under them back until it grows again; a scrolled or moved card goes up again as one strip, at most once a tick while it is still being typed, its band encoded once per set of rows and sent again as it is when only its place changed; it counts as whole, and its trace line says so, only once every row has been seen (ADR-044; `precut_a_tall_card_shows_whole_from_its_first_row_and_typing_changes_nothing`, `precut_a_dashboard_and_the_card_after_it_each_show_whole_at_once`, `precut_rows_not_typed_yet_are_blanked_and_given_back`, `precut_an_interrupted_block_gives_the_harness_lines_under_it_back`, `precut_a_ticking_line_under_the_block_never_cuts_its_card`, `precut_a_block_with_no_room_under_it_shows_its_card_only_whole`, `precut_a_typed_card_that_scrolls_each_row_is_placed_again_once_a_tick`, `precut_no_update_deletes_a_picture_it_replaces`, `precut_a_whole_card_in_two_strips_goes_up_as_one`, `precut_sessions_number_their_pictures_apart`, `precut_a_block_that_waited_for_room_plays_its_entrance_in_one_place`, `precut_a_block_that_waited_for_room_enters_a_tick_after_a_row_typed_in_place`).
- `route-precut-entrance` (pre-cut): `tiles run` sets `TILES_FRAMES` here too; the entrance's frames go to the terminal ahead as images of their own (`a=t`, no placement) within 64 KB a tick; from the card's first showing, on the block's first row, each tick places the frame for `floor(elapsed / 50 ms)` over the whole card, the clock started a frame early so the first showing is frame 1, the first visible one (`a=p`; frame 0 is never sent), and the one before goes a tick later; a late tick skips frames; a frame placed once is never placed again, so a moved or cut card gets its current frame's rows cut fresh and never restarts, and a moved whole card whose frame is over 64 KB places its next frame sent ahead instead, ahead of its turn, never stepping back (ADR-053; `precut_a_dashboard_moved_mid_entrance_goes_on_to_its_next_frame`); the crisp card (`a=T`) ends it and the unplaced frames are deleted. A card shown before its frames are all in the terminal stays settled. `TILES_PRECUT_SEND=tick` sends each frame as it is placed and drops the entrance for a frame over 64 KB (ADR-030).
- `route-motion`: `TILES_MOTION=on|reduce|off`, else macOS Reduce Motion (`defaults read com.apple.universalaccess reduceMotion` prints `1`), else on. On: 10 frames 50 ms apart for one card, dashboard cards 50 ms apart; reduce: `TILES_MOTION=reduce` to the server and 4 opacity-only frames over 150 ms; off: no `TILES_FRAMES`, the settled card pops. `tiles check` prints `pass motion: entrance on the <route> route, 10 frames at 50 ms`, `pass motion: reduce, 4 frames over 150 ms` or `pass motion: off`; `tiles doctor` prints `motion: <setting>, from <source>`.
- `route-sixel` (foot, Windows Terminal): one sixel picture that never reaches the last row.
- `route-text` (Terminal.app, Alacritty, tmux): no wrapper; the result carries the summary and the PNG path. `tiles setup` (when the environment names the terminal) and `tiles doctor` print one `pictures:` line: cards will not draw inline here, Warp, Ghostty, kitty, iTerm2 and WezTerm draw them inline, and here `tiles run` shows them in one window beside the terminal (ADR-046).
- `route-companion-window` (text route, macOS): `tiles run` passes `TILES_SESSION` on the text route too and keeps one window for the session: it opens with the session's first card, beside the terminal's front window (right, else left, else the screen's right edge), and takes each later card of the session in place, keeping its top-left corner while the window fits on its screen and otherwise shifting back inside it; another session's card never shows. It never takes focus (an accessory app's non-activating panel, `orderFrontRegardless`). Closing it leaves the harness running and the next card reopens it; it closes when `tiles run` exits, and ends itself when its file goes or its parent is gone. `TILES_WINDOW=off`, `0`, `false` or `no` keeps it closed. With `TILES_TRACE` the trace gets `window: opened with`, `swapped in`, `closed by the user` and `closed as tiles run ended` lines.
- `route-companion-wording` (text route, macOS): where `tiles run` keeps the companion window it passes `TILES_COMPANION=1` beside `TILES_SESSION`, and the chart result says the user already sees the card in the Tiles window beside the terminal, asks the model to say so in one line without asking the user to open anything, keeps the PNG path for reference and has no `tiles open <id>` line; the session instruction (Claude Code, Codex, the MCP `instructions`, Pi and OpenCode) says the chart is in that window and asks for no PNG path. With the window off, outside `tiles run` or off macOS the result keeps "give the user its path" and the `tiles open` line (ADR-046).
- `route-resize-safe`: a narrowing resize past a wide character, or a one-row pane, makes `vt100` panic; the wrapper contains it, stops drawing cards and passes the harness's output through, and the harness keeps running; when it stops drawing, a card placed early, its entrance included, is removed and the entrance's image deleted (F1, ADR-026).
- `route-own-cards`: `tiles run` hands every wrapped harness its `TILES_SESSION` token on every route, and the wrapper draws a card only when its geometry names it; a block naming a card made for no session or another `tiles run` (an earlier session in scrollback, a second tab, a file the harness shows, a model reply) stays text, and a new card never takes an id already in the cache (ADR-024).
- `route-surround-background`: blanks and card cells take the background of the cell just left of the block (else just right), else the conversation's, remembered from the last screen mostly painted in a colour of its own, never the default there; so a card in a harness that paints its conversation (OpenCode's near-black) sits in no lighter box, leaves no strip, and a block typed from column 0 of a fresh screen never flashes white. Claude Code's default conversation stays on the default.
- `route-flash-sync`: harness bytes written outside an update of the harness's own go into the paint's synchronized update with the blank, so a typed `[tile:` row never shows before its blank (`a_typed_row_never_shows_on_the_default_background_in_a_dark_conversation`); a read that ends inside a sequence is painted at its last ground point, the unfinished sequence after the paint (`a_block_whose_read_ends_mid_sequence_is_painted_in_that_read`), and the next read's rows go in the paint's update after that sequence's end (`a_read_that_begins_mid_sequence_paints_its_rows_in_the_update`), since a block written in one go reached the wrapper in reads split mid-sequence and showed its text for about 30 ms.
- `route-kitty-early` (kitty only): a new card's crisp picture goes to kitty as soon as the card is in the cache, only for a card whose geometry carries this `tiles run` session's `TILES_SESSION` token; its entrance follows as an image of its own, at most 64 KB per 50 ms tick so the harness's output is never held back. On the block's first row the entrance, or the crisp card when the entrance has not arrived, is placed over the rows the block will take, on a backing in the conversation's colour, stopping at the harness's footer, and follows the block when the harness scrolls its first rows off the top; it hands over to the placeholder cells once the block is whole and the entrance has played (ADR-017, ADR-020).
- `route-kitty-entrance` (kitty only): the entrance is one kitty animation at half the card's resolution, its settled frame first and last with the entrance frames between, the cards entering one after another; from its first showing (or the end of the harness's synchronized update) the wrapper shows the frame for the time passed (`a=a,c=N,s=1`), from the first visible one, kitty's frame 2 (entrance frame 0, the card at no opacity, is never sent), skipping any a late tick missed, and stops on the last frame once the entrance's time is up; once handed over the entrance's image is deleted (`a=d,d=I`) and the crisp card stays in its cells. A card shown before its entrance arrived stays settled and its entrance is dropped.
- `route-panel-cut`: no card draws past the conversation's right edge (OpenCode's 42-column sidebar above 120 columns, `Harness::side_panel`) nor past text the harness wrote right of the block's rows; a wider card shows its left part. `tiles run` writes the conversation's width to `cols-<session>` in the cache for the server.
- `route-tall-block`: a block taller than the harness's own scrolling area (OpenCode scrolls under its tab bar, so the block never reaches screen row 0) draws its visible band once all its rows have been seen, and no token stays blank (`a_block_taller_than_the_harness_viewport_draws_its_visible_band`).
- `route-sync-paint`: while the harness holds a synchronized update, the paint waits for its end and goes in just before `?2026l`, inside the update, so it never reads a half-drawn frame.
- `route-fence-blank`: a code fence line (three or more backticks and at most one word, nothing else on the row, at or left of the tokens' column) on the row right above a block's first row or right below any of its rows is blanked on every route, so a block fenced as the model is asked to (ADR-052) shows no ```` ```tiles ```` line around the card in Pi, nor the closing fence Pi draws under the last row typed so far while the block streams (`precut_pi_streamed_closing_fence_is_blanked_left_of_a_growing_card`, ADR-057); a fence a row further away, a row with more than a fence and a bare block's neighbouring text stay as written, and the fence comes back when the block leaves (`a_fence_right_above_and_below_a_block_is_blanked`, `a_fence_or_text_not_right_beside_a_block_stays`, `a_block_with_no_fence_leaves_the_rows_around_it_alone`, `a_fence_the_block_left_gets_its_text_back`, `a_fence_row_from_the_hostile_corpus_adds_no_sequence_of_its_own`; ADR-043).
- `route-precut-resize` (Warp): a resize deletes no pre-cut picture; the next paint places each card again under a new id where its block now stands, and the old picture goes a tick later, so a card neither blinks out nor goes for good under a quiet harness (`precut_a_resize_keeps_the_card_until_it_is_placed_again`, `precut_a_resize_places_the_card_again_where_its_block_stands`, ADR-057).
- `route-precut-wait-blank` (Warp): a block waiting for room or held until its card stops moving has its first row blanked from the token to the end of its title, which stops at a gap of two spaces, within the width its card will be drawn at (cut at a side panel and at text right of the block's other rows), so the wait shows no title in the harness's font, and text past the title or right of that width stays during the wait and after the card goes up, also when the first row comes alone (`precut_a_block_waiting_for_room_shows_no_title`, `precut_a_waiting_block_keeps_the_text_right_of_its_card`, ADR-057).
- `route-write-back`: when a block moves, the cells its placeholders left get back what the harness last wrote there, so no piece of a card stays behind under a harness that redraws only changed cells.
- `route-overrides`: `--terminal`, `--route`, `TILES_TERMINAL`, `TILES_ROUTE`; a bad value is refused naming the valid ones.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- `tiles doctor`, then `tiles run <harness>` in the terminal at hand.

## Driving it with drive.sh

Preconditions:

- `scripts/doctor.sh` passes. The pixel drives need macOS and a kitty binary in `TILES_KITTY`.

- **Cell size and fitting the rows.** `cargo test -p tiles-term a_cell_size_comes_from` covers each cell-size source, Warp's 8x16 and 10x20 points read as 16x32 and 20x40 included; `cargo test -p tiles-mcp a_card_fits_the_rows` draws the six-week column card for 50, 30 and 16 rows and checks it never takes more than the rows less 10, and stays as it was where it fits.
- **Every route, byte for byte.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty` runs the real `tiles run` in a pseudo-terminal per route and checks the startup query, the placeholder cells, the pre-cut placements (`a=T,...,C=1`; one strip, or one per piece the pty hands the block over in, their rows adding up to the card's), the sixel introducer and the unwrapped text route.
- **Cells on the conversation's colour.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty kitty_card_cells` prints the block after `\x1b[48;2;10;10;10m  `; every placeholder row starts `\x1b[0;48;2;10;10;10m`, and none starts on the default `\x1b[0m`.
- **Early card and entrance, byte for byte.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty kitty_places` runs a fake harness that makes its card after `tiles run` starts and types the block's first row 0.4 s before the rest: the crisp card (`a=T,...,U=1`), then the entrance's own image and its frames (`a=t`, `a=f`, `i=5242880`) are written before the first row, the early placement (`p=2`) and a frame after the first visible one (`c=3,s=1` or later, as a late tick may skip) after the first row and before the last, then the stop on frame 10 (`c=10,s=1`), and once the block is whole the early placement's delete, and the settled card alone sent again (`a=T`, `U=1`).
- **The entrance in Warp, byte for byte.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty warp_sends` runs the same fake harness under `--terminal warp`: the frames go out (`a=t`, under an id of the session's own, above `0x01000000`) before the first row, the whole card (`c=<cols>,r=<rows>`) is placed between the first row and the last, at least four placements follow the first row, and the last placement, after the last row, is the crisp card whole (`a=T`, `c=<cols>,r=<rows>`). `cargo test -p tiles-cli precut_` covers the clock, a late tick, a moved card, two cards' clocks, the frame over typed rows and the per-tick fallback byte for byte.
- **Only this session's cards.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty another_sessions_card` runs the real `tiles run` in kitty, Warp and foot with a fake harness that rewrites its card during the run for no session, then for another session's token: the block passes through as text and no kitty or sixel picture is written. Every other pty test stamps its card with the run's own token and draws. `cargo test -p tiles-cli only_this_sessions_cards` writes cards for this session, another and none: only this session's is drawn. `cargo test -p tiles-mcp a_new_card_never_takes` shows a taken id, or a planted link at its path, is refused without being followed.
- **A resize never kills the harness.** `cargo test -p tiles-cli --test pty narrowing` narrows a real pseudo-terminal past a ✅ under `tiles run`; the harness writes its mark and `tiles` exits 0 with `cards stopped drawing` on stderr.

## Gotchas

- `TILES_TRACE=<file>` makes the wrapper log each card's events with their times (in the cache, sent, entrance in the terminal, placed early, entrance frame, stopped, handed over, written back; on pre-cut, entrance queued, entrance starts, rows placed with their frame or settled, entrance over, waiting for room with the rows it needs and has, held until the card stops moving, entrance dropped with the frame and its bytes or another reason, scrolled away, copy lost at a screen row; ADR-053; resized with the cards kept until placed again; ADR-057) and any write to the terminal that took 20 ms or more.
- A tmux pane stands in for no image terminal: under tmux the route is text by design.
- A drive of the companion window under tmux opens a real window on the desktop, since tmux is the text route too; set `TILES_WINDOW=off` for drives that only read the text.
- A Terminal.app first started from a Warp or herdr shell keeps their `WARP_*` and `HERDR_*` variables, and every window it opens is then detected as Warp inside herdr. Unset them in the `.command` file (`for v in ${(k)parameters[(I)(WARP|HERDR)_*]}; do unset $v; done` in zsh) and check `tiles run --dry-run claude` prints `route text` before a real session.
- A shell inside herdr passes `HERDR_*` and `TERM_PROGRAM=herdr` to a kitty window it opens, and the window's `tiles run` then picks the herdr routes: unset them in the session script for a kitty drive.
- Forcing `TILES_CELL_PX` to a size the window does not have shows the settled card narrower than the early one: kitty keeps a placeholder image's aspect but stretches a placed one. Let `tiles run` read the cell size.
- herdr hides the outer terminal (`TERM_PROGRAM=herdr`, XTVERSION `libghostty`), so Tiles picks pre-cut there.
