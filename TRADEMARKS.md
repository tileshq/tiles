# Trademarks

"Tiles" names this project and the official releases Saiem Amer publishes. The licences in `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-design` grant no right to use the name; FSL-1.1-ALv2 says so in its Trademarks section. No mark is registered yet.

## Forks must rename

A fork, or any build you distribute that is not an official release, must go by another name. Do not call it Tiles, put Tiles in its name, or use a name, logo or command that could be taken for Tiles. Change the product name it shows and its binary name.

## What is fine

- Saying your product is "built with Tiles" or "uses Tiles", or that your fork is "based on Tiles", as long as your own name comes first and you do not suggest Tiles made or endorses it.
- Naming Tiles to describe it, compare with it, review it or write about it.
- Keeping the copyright and licence notices that come with the source, as the licence requires.
